## How do you present nothing?

	We spend our careers adding. This talk is about the opposite: what remains when you take everything away, and why it still has to be designed.

## Four kinds of nothing

#### Physics
### Vacuum
	Space with the matter removed. Still full of fields.

#### Mathematics
### Zero
	A number that took a thousand years to accept.

#### Music
### Rest
	Silence written into the score, counted like a note.

#### Typography
### White space
	The part of the page that does the reading for you.

---

## Four rules of subtraction

	Each rule removes one thing. None of them add anything back.

	1. Remove the second typeface
	2. Remove every color but one
	3. Remove the logo from every slide
	4. Remove the slide you were proudest of

---

## Three kinds of nothing

### Absence
	Something that was there and is gone.

### Emptiness
	Room for something that has not arrived.

### Silence
	A pause the audience fills themselves.
