# Write the talk.
### The slides follow.

Welcome to Cue. This is a script, not a slide editor. Write what you want to say, and the slides design themselves.

Paragraphs like this one are speaker notes. Only you see them, in presenter view.

---

## What goes on a slide

	Headings always show. To put any other line on the slide, press Tab at the start of it.

Everything you don't indent stays in your notes. Look at the dot in the left margin of the editor: it marks what the audience will see.

---

## Three rules

### Separate
	Three dashes on their own line start a new slide.

### Show
	Headings, images and tab-indented lines appear on the slide.

### Say
	Everything else is what you say out loud.

When a slide has several subheadings of the same level, they become columns.

## Lists, numbers and code

	1. Write your script in plain text
	2. Indent what the audience should see
	3. Press Present

	```js
	const deck = parse(script);
	present(deck);
	```

---

## Change the design, not the words

	Open **Design** to switch themes, fonts, size, colors, header and footer. Your text never changes, so a new look is a click away.

Settings are stored at the top of the file, so they travel with your markdown.
